**Interpretation**  
For the **original(uncleaned) datasets**, the dimension of pols-month
dataset is 822x9; snp dataset is 11x2; unemployment dataset is of 787x2.

For all three datasets, they all have **the data of date(where later
cleaned as “year”, “month” and “day)**, but can be characterized as
follows:

- the **pols-months** is related to the number of national politicians
  (president, governors, senators, representatives) who are democratic
  or republican at any given **date**

- the **snp** is related to Standard & Poor’s stock market index (S&P),
  showing the closing values of the S&P stock index on the associated
  **date**

- the **unemployment** is related to the percentage of unemployment on
  the associated **month of the year**

While and after **cleaning the data**:

- we removed *day* column from both “pols-months” and “snp” dataset,
  whereas no *day* exists in the “unemployment” dataset. By removing
  *day*, three tables can be better joined together.

- for “snp” dataset, only data from 1950-2015 year are recorded;
  “unemployment” was recorded from 1948-2015; pols” was recorded from
  1947-2015. All missing data from 1947-1949 in “snp” and “unemployment”
  were indicated as “NA” in the resulting dataset.

- we transferred the columns of *Jan* - *Dec* in **unemployment** under
  *month* column for year-month mergability of three tables.

- the cleaned datasets’ dimensions are: pols : 822x9; snp : 787x 3;
  unemployment : 816x 3
